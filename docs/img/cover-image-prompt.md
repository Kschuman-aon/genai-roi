# Cover Image Prompt

Please generate a professional-quality cover image for this textbook.
This image will be used in social media previews and must follow the
formatting guidelines for an Open Graph image preview.

**Required specifications:**
- Format: PNG
- Wide-landscape format
- Size: 1200x630 pixels (1.91:1 aspect ratio)
- This is the Open Graph standard for social media previews

The image has four layers, back to front: background montage, color
treatment, mascot, and title text.

## Subject & Tone

Measuring GenAI ROI is a textbook that teaches practicing IT professionals
(software engineers, data scientists, ML/AI engineers, DevOps/MLOps
practitioners, technical managers) how to measure and communicate the return
on investment of generative AI initiatives — from token economics and cost
optimization through to executive-ready financial reporting. The intended
audience is professional development / adult continuing education for
working technologists, not students. The visual tone should be modern,
professional, and financially credible — closer to a fintech product's
marketing page than a playful classroom textbook — since this book's own
premise is that GenAI cost claims need to be taken seriously by finance
leadership.

## Title

Place "Measuring GenAI ROI" in the center of the image, in a clean, highly
legible sans-serif font. Use a light/white font color with a subtle drop
shadow or dark scrim behind it so it stays readable against the busy montage
background. Keep the title at a large, confident size — simplify the
background directly behind the text rather than shrinking the title to fit.
A smaller subtitle line below it may read "Token Efficiency and Executive
Financial Reporting" in a lighter weight.

## Background Montage

Arrange a montage of the following 7 concepts around the title, each
rendered in a consistent illustration style (see Style below) so the
composition reads as one image rather than a collage of unrelated styles:

- Token — a stream of small glowing rectangular "chip" icons flowing into a
  meter or gauge, suggesting tokens being counted and metered
- Transformer / Neural Network — a glowing layered node-and-edge diagram in
  cool blue tones, suggesting the architecture underneath a language model
- ROI Growth — a clean upward-trending bar chart with a dollar sign
  integrated into one bar, on a dark dashboard-style panel
- GPU Compute — a stylized data-center server rack or GPU chip glowing
  amber/orange, suggesting the hardware cost layer beneath every model
- Vector Embeddings — a cluster of small glowing points connected in 3D
  space, suggesting a vector database / semantic search
- Executive Report — a clean tablet or dashboard mockup showing a simple bar
  chart and a one-line financial summary, suggesting the executive-facing
  deliverable
- Risk Shield — a minimal shield icon with a small checkmark or lock,
  suggesting governance and risk management

## Mascot

Place the book's mascot, Ledger the Fox, in the lower-left corner, sized so
it does not overlap the title text. Ledger is a modern flat-vector
illustration of a fox with warm rust-orange fur, small round wire-frame
glasses, a slim indigo necktie, and a thin brown leather strap suggesting a
ledger book at the hip — professional and sharp-eyed, not cartoonish or
childish. Use the reference image at docs/img/mascot/welcome.png (Ledger
waving) for exact appearance and pose.

## Style & Composition

- Illustration style: flat vector / modern digital illustration — choose
  one style and apply it to every montage element for visual consistency.
- Color palette: deep indigo and dark navy as the dominant background,
  with rust-orange and amber accents (matching the book's theme palette
  and Ledger's own coloring).
- Lighting/mood: confident and polished, subtle glow effects on the
  digital/data elements (tokens, embeddings, GPU) rather than flat matte
  color, evoking a premium fintech or enterprise-software product.
- Composition: title centered, montage elements arranged in a loose ring
  or grid around it, Ledger in the lower-left, generous negative space
  immediately behind the title so it stays readable.

## Avoid

- Do not render dense paragraphs of illegible text anywhere in the image.
- Avoid generic stock-photo cliches (handshakes, isolated lightbulbs,
  people pointing at whiteboards) — none of those are among the chosen
  concepts.
- Avoid photorealistic human faces — this book has no human characters,
  only Ledger.
- Do not let montage elements visually compete with or overlap the title
  or Ledger.
- Avoid a playful/childish tone (rounded bubble fonts, pastel rainbow
  colors, classroom clip-art) — this book is aimed at working professionals
  and finance-literate executives, not a K-12 audience.
