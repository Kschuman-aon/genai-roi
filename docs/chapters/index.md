# Chapters

This textbook is organized into 27 chapters covering 561 concepts.

## Chapter Overview

1. [Core Concepts of Large Language Models](01-core-concepts-llms/index.md) - Introduces the vocabulary of generative AI systems — AI, machine learning, deep learning, transformers, and large language models — building the conceptual foundation every later cost discussion depends on.
2. [Prompting, Deployment, and Model Optimization Basics](02-prompting-deployment-optimization/index.md) - Covers how prompts are engineered and models are deployed and optimized, from retrieval-augmented generation and hallucination through quantization and right-sizing.
3. [Tokenization and Token Pricing Fundamentals](03-tokenization-pricing-fundamentals/index.md) - Explains how text becomes tokens and how providers count and price them, establishing the vocabulary needed to read any generative AI usage bill.
4. [Managing and Benchmarking Token Consumption](04-managing-benchmarking-tokens/index.md) - Shows how prompt length, caching, chunking, and multi-turn conversations drive token consumption, and how to benchmark and compare token efficiency across providers.
5. [Compute Pricing and Scaling Fundamentals](05-compute-pricing-scaling/index.md) - Surveys the compute pricing models beneath every generative AI bill — on-demand, reserved, spot, and serverless — along with autoscaling and utilization tradeoffs.
6. [Infrastructure Planning and Cost Monitoring](06-infrastructure-planning-monitoring/index.md) - Moves from serving infrastructure and hardware accelerators to capacity planning, multi-region deployment, and the dashboards used to monitor infrastructure spend.
7. [Prompt, Routing, and Retrieval Efficiency Techniques](07-prompt-routing-retrieval-efficiency/index.md) - Presents prompt optimization, model cascading and routing, and retrieval-tuning techniques that reduce token and compute cost without sacrificing quality.
8. [Model Compression, Testing, and Continuous Efficiency](08-model-compression-testing-efficiency/index.md) - Covers testing-driven efficiency techniques — A/B testing, benchmarking, distillation, quantization, and speculative decoding — plus the continuous-optimization discipline that sustains savings.
9. [Core Financial and ROI Vocabulary](09-core-financial-roi-vocabulary/index.md) - Builds the core financial vocabulary a technologist needs before writing any ROI report: ROI, TCO, NPV, payback period, and basic cost-accounting terms.
10. [Business Case Development and Financial Forecasting](10-business-case-financial-forecasting/index.md) - Extends financial fundamentals into unit economics, business-case development, investment appraisal, and the forecasting techniques used to project financial outcomes.
11. [Productivity and Quality Metrics for GenAI](11-productivity-quality-metrics/index.md) - Introduces the metrics that connect generative AI usage to productivity and quality outcomes, from adoption rate through developer velocity and defect reduction.
12. [Measurement Rigor and KPI Reporting](12-measurement-rigor-kpi-reporting/index.md) - Covers accuracy-oriented metrics and the measurement rigor — baselines, control groups, statistical significance — needed to trust any reported KPI.
13. [Telemetry, Logging, and Cost Dashboards](13-telemetry-logging-cost-dashboards/index.md) - Explains how usage and cost telemetry is captured through logging, tracing, and observability platforms, culminating in real-time cost dashboards.
14. [Cost Attribution, Governance, and Observability Maturity](14-cost-attribution-observability-maturity/index.md) - Covers cost attribution across users, features, and departments, plus the data governance and instrumentation-maturity practices that keep cost data trustworthy.
15. [AI-Assisted Coding Across the SDLC](15-ai-assisted-coding-sdlc/index.md) - Traces cost impact across the software development lifecycle as AI assists with requirements, code generation, review, and testing.
16. [SDLC Productivity, Risk, and Cost Roadmapping](16-sdlc-productivity-risk-roadmapping/index.md) - Covers developer-productivity metrics, the risks of AI-assisted coding, and the roadmapping techniques used to plan and report SDLC cost reduction.
17. [Data and Model Lifecycle Cost Drivers](17-data-model-lifecycle-cost-drivers/index.md) - Follows generative AI's cost impact through the data science lifecycle, from data collection and labeling through model training, evaluation, and drift detection.
18. [MLOps Infrastructure and Reuse Economics](18-mlops-infrastructure-reuse-economics/index.md) - Covers the MLOps infrastructure — feature stores, model registries, and deployment testing patterns — and reuse strategies that reduce data science cost.
19. [Vendor Pricing and Licensing Economics](19-vendor-pricing-licensing-economics/index.md) - Analyzes vendor and model pricing structures, licensing models, and the total-cost frameworks used to compare vendor offers on equal footing.
20. [Vendor Selection and Sourcing Strategy](20-vendor-selection-sourcing-strategy/index.md) - Covers structured vendor-selection methods — benchmarking, pilots, due diligence, and scorecards — alongside the sourcing risks that accompany vendor decisions.
21. [Governance, Compliance, and Security Risk](21-governance-compliance-security-risk/index.md) - Surfaces governance, compliance, and security risks that do not appear on a token invoice but materially affect a generative AI system's true cost.
22. [Risk-Adjusted Costing and Executive Risk Reporting](22-risk-adjusted-costing-reporting/index.md) - Covers risk-adjusted costing frameworks and the practices used to register, mitigate, and report AI-related risk to executive stakeholders.
23. [Audience Analysis and Executive Framing](23-audience-analysis-executive-framing/index.md) - Introduces the audience-analysis and message-framing skills needed to translate technical cost findings into terms a financial executive will act on.
24. [Presenting and Defending Findings to Executives](24-presenting-defending-findings/index.md) - Covers presentation design, handling uncertainty and objections, and the credibility-building techniques used to defend findings before an executive audience.
25. [Designing the Executive ROI Report](25-designing-executive-roi-report/index.md) - Walks through the structural mechanics of an executive ROI report: scope definition, methodology, findings, recommendations, appendices, and front matter.
26. [Data Visualization and Storytelling for Financial Audiences](26-data-visualization-storytelling/index.md) - Covers chart selection and design for financial data — bar, line, waterfall, sensitivity, and scenario visualizations — built for a non-technical audience.
27. [Report Production, Review, and the Capstone Project](27-report-production-review-capstone/index.md) - Combines the quality-assurance pass on a finished report with the step-by-step capstone project: building, critiquing, and presenting a complete GenAI ROI report.

## How to Use This Textbook

Work through the chapters in order: each chapter's concepts depend only on concepts introduced in the same or an earlier chapter, so the numbering reflects a valid learning path. Readers with prior experience in a given area (e.g., generative AI foundations, or corporate finance) may skip ahead, but should verify prerequisite concepts from earlier chapters are already familiar.
