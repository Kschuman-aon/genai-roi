# List of MicroSims for Measuring GenAI ROI

Interactive Micro Simulations to help students learn measuring genai roi fundamentals.

<div class="grid cards" markdown>

-   **[Anatomy of a Prompt](./prompt-anatomy-explorer/index.md)**

    ![Anatomy of a Prompt](./prompt-anatomy-explorer/prompt-anatomy-explorer.png)

    Interactive p5.js MicroSim for anatomy of a prompt.

-   **[Autoscaling and Warm Pool Behavior Over a Traffic Day](./autoscaling-traffic-simulator/index.md)**

    ![Autoscaling and Warm Pool Behavior Over a Traffic Day](./autoscaling-traffic-simulator/autoscaling-traffic-simulator.png)

    Interactive p5.js MicroSim for autoscaling and warm pool behavior over a traffic day.

-   **[Benefit Realization Calculator](./benefit-realization-calculator/index.md)**

    ![Benefit Realization Calculator](./benefit-realization-calculator/benefit-realization-calculator.png)

    The learner will calculate the year 1 realized benefit and the three-year ROI of the running example from measured adoption, usage coverage, minutes saved, and rework data, to within $1 for dollar values and 0.1 percentage points for ROI.

-   **[Budget Burn Alert Simulator](./budget-burn-alert-simulator/index.md)**

    ![Budget Burn Alert Simulator](./budget-burn-alert-simulator/budget-burn-alert-simulator.png)

    The learner will calculate the day on which each of four cost alerts (50%, 80%, 100% of budget, and month-end forecast) fires for a month with a runaway daily spend, to the exact day.

-   **[Byte Pair Encoding Step-Through](./bpe-tokenizer-explorer/index.md)**

    ![Byte Pair Encoding Step-Through](./bpe-tokenizer-explorer/bpe-tokenizer-explorer.png)

    Interactive p5.js MicroSim for byte pair encoding step-through.

-   **[Compute Utilization and Idle Cost Explorer](./compute-utilization-explorer/index.md)**

    ![Compute Utilization and Idle Cost Explorer](./compute-utilization-explorer/compute-utilization-explorer.png)

    Interactive Chart.js MicroSim for compute utilization and idle cost explorer.

-   **[Context Window Budget Allocation](./context-window-budget-chart/index.md)**

    ![Context Window Budget Allocation](./context-window-budget-chart/context-window-budget-chart.png)

    Interactive Chart.js MicroSim for context window budget allocation.

-   **[Cost Concept Classifier](./cost-concept-classifier/index.md)**

    ![Cost Concept Classifier](./cost-concept-classifier/cost-concept-classifier.png)

    The learner will classify each of eight spending descriptions as capital expenditure, operating expenditure, sunk cost, or opportunity cost.

-   **[Cost Per Task Benchmark Explorer](./cost-per-task-benchmark-explorer/index.md)**

    ![Cost Per Task Benchmark Explorer](./cost-per-task-benchmark-explorer/cost-per-task-benchmark-explorer.png)

    The learner will compare the cost per completed task of a small and a large model, including human review of rejected answers, and identify which is cheaper at four review costs.

-   **[Cross-Provider Token Efficiency Dashboard](./token-efficiency-dashboard/index.md)**

    ![Cross-Provider Token Efficiency Dashboard](./token-efficiency-dashboard/token-efficiency-dashboard.png)

    Interactive Chart.js MicroSim for cross-provider token efficiency dashboard.

-   **[Deployment Placement Sorter](./deployment-placement-sorter/index.md)**

    ![Deployment Placement Sorter](./deployment-placement-sorter/deployment-placement-sorter.png)

    The learner will distinguish which of four placement approaches (single region, multi-region, edge, hybrid cloud) best fits each of eight workload descriptions, by matching the deciding requirement in each description.

-   **[Fixed Versus Variable Break-Even Calculator](./break-even-calculator/index.md)**

    ![Fixed Versus Variable Break-Even Calculator](./break-even-calculator/break-even-calculator.png)

    The learner will calculate the monthly request volume at which a fixed-cost server and a per-request API cost the same, to within 1,000 requests.

-   **[From Raw Text to Helpful Assistant](./training-lifecycle-workflow/index.md)**

    ![From Raw Text to Helpful Assistant](./training-lifecycle-workflow/training-lifecycle-workflow.png)

    Interactive Mermaid MicroSim for from raw text to helpful assistant.

-   **[Metric Matcher](./metric-matcher/index.md)**

    ![Metric Matcher](./metric-matcher/metric-matcher.png)

    The learner will classify each of ten described situations under the one metric from a list of eight that the situation measures, with at least 8 of 10 correct on the first attempt.

-   **[Model Cascade Cost Calculator](./model-cascade-cost-calculator/index.md)**

    ![Model Cascade Cost Calculator](./model-cascade-cost-calculator/model-cascade-cost-calculator.png)

    The learner will calculate the monthly cost of a small-model-first cascade for a stated pass rate and decide whether it is cheaper than sending every request to the large model.

-   **[Model Scale Across Five Years](./model-parameter-scale-chart/index.md)**

    ![Model Scale Across Five Years](./model-parameter-scale-chart/model-parameter-scale-chart.png)

    Interactive Chart.js MicroSim for model scale across five years.

-   **[NPV and Payback Calculator](./npv-payback-calculator/index.md)**

    ![NPV and Payback Calculator](./npv-payback-calculator/npv-payback-calculator.png)

    The learner will calculate the net present value of the running-example cash flows at a stated discount rate to within $5, and decide whether the investment should be accepted.

-   **[One-Time Training vs. Recurring Inference](./training-vs-inference-cost-chart/index.md)**

    ![One-Time Training vs. Recurring Inference](./training-vs-inference-cost-chart/training-vs-inference-cost-chart.png)

    Interactive Chart.js MicroSim for one-time training vs. recurring inference.

-   **[Peak Capacity Planner](./peak-capacity-planner/index.md)**

    ![Peak Capacity Planner](./peak-capacity-planner/peak-capacity-planner.png)

    The learner will calculate the number of inference servers needed for a stated peak request rate and utilization ceiling, and the monthly cost of provisioning for the peak around the clock, to the nearest whole server and dollar.

-   **[Per-Request Token Cost Calculator](./token-cost-calculator/index.md)**

    ![Per-Request Token Cost Calculator](./token-cost-calculator/token-cost-calculator.png)

    Interactive p5.js MicroSim for per-request token cost calculator.

-   **[Prompt A/B Test Decision Lab](./prompt-ab-test-decision-lab/index.md)**

    ![Prompt A/B Test Decision Lab](./prompt-ab-test-decision-lab/prompt-ab-test-decision-lab.png)

    The learner will judge, from a 95% interval for the quality difference, whether to adopt, reject, or keep testing a shorter prompt variant against a 3-point quality tolerance.

-   **[Prompt Compression Before/After Comparator](./prompt-compression-comparator/index.md)**

    ![Prompt Compression Before/After Comparator](./prompt-compression-comparator/prompt-compression-comparator.png)

    Interactive p5.js MicroSim for prompt compression before/after comparator.

-   **[Request Routing Flow](./request-routing-flow/index.md)**

    ![Request Routing Flow](./request-routing-flow/request-routing-flow.png)

    The learner will explain which path through cache, small model, quality check, large model, and fallback model each of five example requests takes and what it costs.

-   **[Same Task, Three Prompting Strategies](./prompting-technique-comparator/index.md)**

    ![Same Task, Three Prompting Strategies](./prompting-technique-comparator/prompting-technique-comparator.png)

    Interactive p5.js MicroSim for same task, three prompting strategies.

-   **[Self-Attention, Qualitatively](./self-attention-explorer/index.md)**

    ![Self-Attention, Qualitatively](./self-attention-explorer/self-attention-explorer.png)

    Interactive p5.js MicroSim for self-attention, qualitatively.

-   **[Semantic Cache Threshold Explorer](./semantic-cache-threshold-explorer/index.md)**

    ![Semantic Cache Threshold Explorer](./semantic-cache-threshold-explorer/semantic-cache-threshold-explorer.png)

    The learner will recommend the similarity threshold that gives the largest saving without exceeding a stated tolerance for wrong cached answers per 100,000 requests.

-   **[Sensitivity Tornado Ranker](./sensitivity-tornado-ranker/index.md)**

    ![Sensitivity Tornado Ranker](./sensitivity-tornado-ranker/sensitivity-tornado-ranker.png)

    The learner will differentiate which of four input changes reduces the running-example NPV the most, by ranking them from most to least damaging, and will predict whether a combined change makes the NPV negative.

-   **[Serving Batch Size Tuner](./serving-batch-tuner/index.md)**

    ![Serving Batch Size Tuner](./serving-batch-tuner/serving-batch-tuner.png)

    The learner will solve for the batch size that gives the lowest cost per million tokens while a 400-token response finishes within a stated latency limit and the batch fits in GPU memory.

-   **[Speed Versus Quality Check](./speed-versus-quality-check/index.md)**

    ![Speed Versus Quality Check](./speed-versus-quality-check/speed-versus-quality-check.png)

    The learner will judge, for each of eight time-saving claims, whether the claim can be reported as stated or must be restated net of rework, using the rule that rework minutes above 25% of the claimed saving require restatement.

-   **[The AI Family Tree](./ai-family-tree/index.md)**

    ![The AI Family Tree](./ai-family-tree/ai-family-tree.png)

    An interactive vis-network diagram showing how Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Deep Learning, Neural Networks, Generative AI, Foundation Models, and Large Language Models nest inside one another.
